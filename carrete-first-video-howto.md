# Carrete — How to Build Your First Videos (step by step)

This operationalizes the storyboard + free workflow into something you can do **this week**. Free path: **Kling** (free tier — our chosen tool; see the tool-decision doc) for one cinematic opener + the venue's real footage + CapCut (free desktop) to assemble. Target: a finished 9:16 reel, ~18s, no watermark.

## Setup (once, ~30 min)
1. **CapCut desktop (free)** — download for Mac/Windows. This is your editor, captions, voiceover, and music in one. (Use *desktop*, not mobile — desktop free export has no watermark.)
2. **Kling account (free)** — no card. Free tier gives ~66 credits/day (≈ a couple of 5s clips) with image-to-video, camera-motion presets + Motion Brush, and 9:16. Free output is 720p + watermarked + non-commercial — perfect for trials (see "when to pay").
3. **Pexels / Pixabay** — bookmark for free, commercial-safe filler B-roll if the venue's footage is thin.

## The per-reel pipeline (~60–90 min per reel, first few slower)

**Step 0 — Get the assets.** Send the venue the "Pídele al restaurante" shot list from the storyboard (8–12 phone photos/clips: hero dish, 2–3 other dishes, interior, a person/hands moment, exterior + logo). They shoot it on a phone in 10 minutes and send via WhatsApp.

**Step 1 — Generate the opener (Kling, ~10 min + render).**
- Pick the single best still (the hero dish or the storefront).
- Image-to-video: upload the still, **set 9:16**, pick a **camera-motion preset** (slow push-in or orbit) — or paint the move with **Motion Brush** — **5 seconds**, free/standard model.
- Use the storyboard's prompt. Iterate at low settings first; only spend credits on the "final."
- Download it. **Hide the watermark**: in CapCut, scale the clip up ~5–8% or drop your Carrete logo over the watermark corner.

**Step 2 — Assemble in CapCut (~30–45 min).**
- New 9:16 project. Drop the **AI opener first** (0–3s hook), then sequence the real clips per the storyboard timing.
- Trim each clip to ~1.5–2.5s — fast cuts hold attention.
- Add **on-screen text** for the hook and the closer (storyboard copy), styled in your brand font/colors.
- **Auto-captions** for any spoken/voiceover lines (CapCut does 20+ languages).
- Optional **AI voiceover** if the reel needs a line.

**Step 3 — Music + rhythm (~10 min).**
- Add a **trending/royalty-free track** from CapCut's library. Put cuts **on the beat** — this single thing separates pro from amateur.
- Keep volume balanced; duck music under any voiceover.

**Step 4 — Export.** 1080p, 9:16, 30fps, MP4. Confirm no watermark.

## The quality bar (QC checklist before you send it)
- [ ] Hook lands in the **first 1 second** (text + striking visual).
- [ ] Total length **≤ 20s**.
- [ ] Cuts are **on the beat**; nothing lingers > ~3s.
- [ ] Captions are **legible** on mobile (big, high-contrast, safe from edges).
- [ ] Ends with a clear **CTA** (reserva / link in bio / oferta).
- [ ] **AI-assisted content labeled** where the platform requires it.
- [ ] Watermark gone; export is crisp.

## Weekly cadence (two founders, free tier)
- Kling's free tier resets ~66 credits/day → **a couple of openers/day, easily ~5–7/week.** Rotate which trial reel gets the AI opener; pad the rest with real footage + Pexels. (Free credits are trial-only — watermark/non-commercial.)
- **Founder B** runs Steps 1–4 (production). **Founder A** runs Step 0 (sourcing/sending) and posts/sends delivery.
- Realistic output: **5 trial reels in week 1** — exactly enough for the validation sprint.

## When to start paying (and what it buys)
The moment a venue says "yes, here's money," upgrade **Kling Standard (~€6.99/mo)**: removes the watermark, unlocks **1080p + commercial license + better models** and longer clips. It pays for itself on the first paid reel and removes the free-tier licensing gray area. Keep a free **Hailuo** account as overflow/backup for variety.

## First action
Make **one** reel end-to-end this week using the storyboard. That single asset is your portfolio, your sales demo, and proof the workflow holds — before you commit to producing for five venues.
