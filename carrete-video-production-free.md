# Carrete — Free / Near-Free Video Production with Higgsfield

*Researched June 2026. Exact credit numbers come from cross-verified third-party reviews — Higgsfield's pricing page is JS-rendered and changes often, so treat figures as "approximately."*

## Higgsfield's free tier — the honest reality

There's a genuine "forever free" plan, but it's small:

- **~10 credits/day** (resets daily), **standard/free models only** (no Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0), **1 concurrent job**, **720p**, and a **Higgsfield watermark**. No card required.
- A 5s clip at 720p costs ~6 credits → roughly **1 usable clip per day**.
- **Commercial-use is restricted on the free tier** — risky for billing clients with free-tier output. Verify the ToS before using free clips in paid work.
- Referral/promo credits exist but are unreliable — don't build a plan on them.

**Verdict:** free is great for testing, learning prompts, and building a demo reel — **not** for paid client volume.

## What Higgsfield is actually good at

- **Image-to-video** from a real still (its core strength).
- **Cinema Studio camera presets** — crash zoom, dolly, orbit, push-in, FPV — its real differentiator; no rival matches the cinematic-opener look.
- **9:16 vertical** fully supported (lock the aspect ratio before generating, or you waste credits).
- Clips of **3–5s** on free models, 720p/30fps MP4.

## The free production pipeline (9:16 reels, 15–25s)

The trick: **Higgsfield supplies 1–2 AI "wow" moments; everything else is real footage + free tools.**

1. **Collect the owner's real photos/clips** (dishes, gym floor, hotel room). Real footage is the backbone — authentic and free.
2. **Pick ONE hero still** for the cinematic opener (best dish, the storefront).
3. **Higgsfield (free, ~1/day):** image-to-video on that still — 9:16, a Cinema Studio preset (slow push-in/orbit), 5s. This is your scroll-stopping 0–3s opener. Iterate prompts at low settings before the "final."
4. **Handle the watermark legitimately:** crop slightly or place your logo/text overlay over the watermark zone (it's your own generation, so cropping is fine).
5. **Assemble in CapCut (free desktop):** drop the AI opener first, then 4–6 real clips. CapCut free gives no-watermark desktop export, auto-captions (20+ languages), AI text-to-speech voiceover, free music/SFX, and 9:16 auto-reframe — your editor, captioner, and voiceover in one.
6. **Stretch credits across the week:** rotate which client gets the AI opener each day. ~7 free clips/week ≈ one cinematic opener for each of ~5–7 reels, padded with real footage + free stock.

### Free companion stack
- **Edit / captions / voiceover / reframe:** CapCut free (desktop).
- **Free stock B-roll:** Pexels, Pixabay (commercial-safe).
- **Overflow AI video (when 10 credits run out):** PixVerse (~60 credits/day, watermarked), Kling (~66/day), Hailuo (3–5/day), Pika (80/mo, no watermark), or **Google Veo via AI Studio** (watermark-free).

### Realistic free output
**~5–7 finished reels/week** — demo-grade. Good for portfolio, free trials, low-stakes posting. Not clean enough (watermark, 720p, license gray area) for paid delivery at volume.

### What ~$9–17/month would unlock (balanced view)
- **~$9/mo:** ~150–200 credits, **no watermark, 1080p, commercial license**, 2 concurrent jobs → 5–10 clean client clips/month.
- **~$17/mo Pro:** ~600 credits + premium models (Sora 2, Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0) + longer clips — the real production threshold.

**Recommendation:** run the free path to learn and build a demo reel. The moment you land a paying client, the $9–17/mo tier **pays for itself in one reel** and removes the watermark/license risk.
