# Carrete — Which AI Video Tool (decision)

**Verdict: Kling is the tool.** Free tier to start, **Kling Standard (~€6.99/mo)** the moment a client pays. Keep a free **Hailuo** account as overflow/backup. Higgsfield drops to *optional* (nice preset UX, but it's mostly a wrapper over the same models).

## Why — judged on the ONE criterion that matters here

Carrete animates a **real photo of a specific dish/room/storefront**. So the deciding factor is **image-to-video fidelity on a real still + camera-motion control** — not text-to-video imagination. On that axis:

| Tool | Real-photo fidelity | Camera motion | 9:16 / res | Free tier | Paid entry | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kling** | **Best subject preservation** | 20+ presets + Motion Brush | ✅ 1080p | ~66 cr/day, 720p, watermark, non-commercial | **€6.99/mo**: no watermark, 1080p, commercial | ✅ **Winner** |
| Google Veo 3.1 | Highest raw photorealism | Prompt-only (no presets) | ✅ up to 4K | limited | $19.99/mo | Top quality, budget-stretcher, no presets |
| Higgsfield | = underlying model | **70+ presets** (best UX) | ✅ 1080p | 10 cr/day, watermark | $15/mo for commercial | Optional polish only |
| Hailuo/MiniMax | Strong motion/physics | Prompt-driven | ✅ 1080p | 200 cr (expire 3 days) | $7.99/mo | ✅ **Backup/overflow** |
| Runway Gen-4.x | Good; best control tooling | Director Mode + Motion Brush | ✅ 1080p | 125 one-time cr | $12/mo | Pricey, credits burn fast |
| PixVerse | Decent for price | Camera + keyframes | ✅ 720p paid | 60 cr/day, 540p | $10/mo | Cheap alt |
| Pika | Weakest on real photos | Effects, not camera | 480p free | 80 cr/mo, 480p | ~$8/mo | ❌ Skip (480p free) |

## The plan
- **Phase 1 (free / validation):** Kling free tier for the cinematic opener (iterate prompts cheaply; free output is trial-only — 720p, watermark, non-commercial). Hailuo free as overflow when daily credits run out.
- **Phase 2 (first paying client):** Kling Standard ~€6.99/mo → watermark-free 1080p + commercial license. Add Hailuo $7.99 only if you need overflow/variety; combined still ~€15/mo.
- **Skip:** Pika (480p free cap). **Optional:** Higgsfield only if you love its one-click orbit/dolly preset UX.

## If you only pick one tool: **Kling.**
Best balance of real-photo fidelity, camera-motion presets + Motion Brush, 9:16/1080p, and a €6.99 commercial tier — the cheapest path from free trials to paid client delivery without changing tools.
