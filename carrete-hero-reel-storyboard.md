# Carrete — Hero Sample Reel Storyboard

**Format:** 9:16 vertical · ~18s · Restaurant vertical · Spanish market
**Production:** Higgsfield free (1 opener clip) + owner's real phone footage/photos + Pexels/Pixabay stock fallback · Assembled in CapCut free (desktop)
**Goal:** The calling card. Make a small restaurant look like it has a Michelin marketing budget. Then end on a line that makes other owners want this for *their* place.

---

## 1. Concept + Hook

**Concept — "De tu móvil a esto":** We take the exact footage any restaurant already has on their phone (a plate, the dining room, a pour) and show how Carrete turns it into something cinematic. The reel is *the proof and the pitch in one*. The first second is a hyper-appetizing, slow-motion cinematic shot of a dish that stops the scroll cold, then the energy builds with quick cuts of the venue, and lands on the value line: *"Tu negocio merece verse así."*

**The 0–1s hook:**
- **Visual:** Extreme close-up, slow push-in on a steaming/glistening dish (the Higgsfield-animated still). Subtle motion — steam rising, a slow camera creep. This is the "food porn" frame that beats the algorithm.
- **Text (appears ~0:00):** **"Esto es un restaurante de tu barrio."** (white, bold, centered-lower).
- **Why it stops the thumb:** Vertical food close-ups with motion are the single highest-retention opener on Reels/TikTok in Spain. The text creates a pattern-interrupt — viewers expect a glossy ad, then learn it's a *local neighborhood spot*, which makes them watch to see "how." Hunger + curiosity in one second.

## 3. Exact Higgsfield Prompt(s)

**Setup in Higgsfield (free tier):** Upload the owner's real dish photo → Image-to-Video → Standard model → Camera preset: **Cinema Studio** → Aspect ratio **9:16** → Duration **5s** → 720p. Generate 2 variants, keep the best.

**Primary opener prompt (copy-paste):**
```
Cinematic slow push-in on the plated dish, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic, shallow depth of field,
gentle rising steam, warm golden restaurant lighting, subtle glistening reflections on the food,
ultra-detailed appetizing close-up, smooth cinematic camera dolly forward, 5 seconds, film grain,
shot on cinema camera. Camera preset: Cinema Studio.
```

**Backup / alternate (if the dish is dry, e.g. dessert or charcuterie):**
```
Cinematic slow orbit around the plated dish, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic, shallow depth of field,
soft warm ambient restaurant glow, delicate highlights catching the food texture, slow elegant
camera arc, premium food-film aesthetic, 5 seconds, subtle film grain. Camera preset: Cinema Studio.
```

> **Tips:** Pick the dish photo that's already sharp and well-lit — Higgsfield amplifies whatever it's given. Avoid prompts naming a specific cuisine/brand; you're animating *their* real plate, so let the photo carry the identity. The watermark sits at a screen edge — see the crop trick in step 4.

## 4. CapCut Assembly Steps (free, desktop)

1. **New project → set canvas to 9:16** (1080×1920). Top-right aspect ratio selector.
2. **Import everything:** Higgsfield clip, all real phone photos/clips, any Pexels/Pixabay stock. Drag into the media bin.
3. **Hide the Higgsfield watermark (crop trick):** Select the AI clip → **Edit → Crop**, scale up ~108–112% so the watermarked edge pushes off-frame; recenter on the dish. Alternatively, drop a small solid-color shape or your Carrete logo over the watermark corner. Verify on full-screen preview.
4. **Lay the sequence** on the timeline in the order from the table. Trim each clip to its time slot. Use the AI opener first (~3s), then real footage.
5. **Add music FIRST, then cut to it** (see step 6 / section 6). Drop the track on the audio lane, find the beats, and snap your cut points to the beat markers (the cuts at 0:03 / 0:06 / 0:08 / 0:10 should land *on* beats).
6. **Transitions:** Keep it punchy — mostly hard cuts. One subtle *zoom/whip* between the montage cuts (0:10–0:13) max. Don't over-transition; cuts read as more premium.
7. **Speed:** Slightly slow the pour and the final hero shot (0.8×) for a cinematic feel. Speed up any montage clip that drags.
8. **Auto-captions / text:**
   - For on-screen lines, use **Text → Add text**, font **bold sans-serif** (e.g. "Aa" default bold), white with a subtle dark shadow or 40% black drop for legibility.
   - Keep text in the safe zone (not under the Reels UI — keep ~15% margin top and bottom).
   - Animate text in with a quick **Fade** or **Rise** (in-animation ~0.3s). No cheesy effects.
   - If using AI voiceover (optional): **Text-to-speech → Spanish (Spain) voice**, only for the end line if at all. The reel works fully music-only.
9. **Color:** Apply a light **filter** (warm/"Food" or "Warm" LUT) at ~20–30% strength across all clips so the real phone footage matches the cinematic AI opener. This consistency is what sells the quality.
10. **End card (0:16–0:18):** Solid background (deep warm tone or black), Carrete name large, @handle below, tagline *"Tu negocio merece verse así."*, small dish thumbnail in a corner.
11. **Export:** Resolution **1080p**, frame rate **30fps**, format MP4. Confirm **no CapCut watermark** (CapCut free desktop exports clean; if a watermark template was used, remove it). Bitrate: Recommended/High.

## 6. Music Guidance

- **Type:** A trending, upbeat-but-warm track with a clear **beat drop around the 2–3s mark** — something with momentum that builds, not lyrical/distracting. Think "satisfying / aesthetic / foodie" audio, or a current viral instrumental with a punchy kick. Avoid anything aggressive or overly EDM; the vibe is *appetizing and premium*.
- **How to pick in CapCut:** Open **Audio → Music → search "trending" / "vlog" / "food" / "aesthetic"**, or browse the **TikTok/CapCut trending** category. Preview, and pick one where the **beat hits line up naturally with your cut points** (0:03, 0:06, 0:08, 0:10).
- **Beat-sync trick:** With the track on the timeline, right-click → **Add beat markers** (or manually tap-to-mark). Then snap each clip's cut to a marker. This single step is what makes the reel feel professionally edited.
- **For Instagram:** CapCut library music is safe for export, but to maximize reach on Reels, you can also **re-add a trending audio inside Instagram** after uploading (mix your CapCut track low + IG trending audio), since IG's algorithm boosts native trending sounds. Keep your beat-synced edit regardless.

## 7. "Pídele al restaurante" Shot List

Send this to the venue — they shoot it on a phone in ~10 minutes. **Horizontal? No — tell them to film VERTICAL (9:16), hold steady, tap to focus, and shoot in daylight or warm evening light.** Aim for 3–5 seconds per clip.

1. **Foto del plato estrella** — bien iluminada, cenital o en ángulo, sin desorden alrededor. *(This is the one we animate in Higgsfield — make it the best photo.)*
2. **Clip del comedor / sala** — paneo lento mostrando el ambiente, mesas puestas, luz cálida.
3. **Clip de un camarero/cocinero emplatando** — manos trabajando, un detalle final (una pizca de sal, una hoja).
4. **Clip de "el chorro"** — servir una copa de vino / vermut / café, a cámara lenta natural.
5. **Foto/clip de la fachada o el cartel** — la entrada del local, identificable.
6. **Clip de un detalle de ingrediente** — algo fresco (tomate, pan recién hecho, jamón) en primer plano.
7. **Clip de mesa desde arriba** — varios platos juntos, manos compartiendo comida.
8. **Clip o foto del dueño/equipo sonriendo** — pone cara humana al negocio.
9. **Clip de un cliente disfrutando** (con permiso) — alguien riendo o brindando.
10. **Detalle de textura** — corte de un plato, queso fundido, postre. *(opcional)*
11. **Logo del restaurante** en alta resolución (PNG si lo tienen). *(para el cierre)*
12. **Foto general del local lleno/animado** *(opcional, refuerza prueba social)*

> Minimum viable set if they're busy: **#1 (dish photo), #2 (interior clip), #3 or #4 (action/pour), #5 (facade), #11 (logo).** Everything else is upside.

## 8. Quick Variants

**Boutique Hotel**
- Hook (0–1s): Higgsfield slow push-in on a styled bed / suite window with morning light spilling in. Text: **"Esto es un hotel en Madrid."**
- Beats: room → rooftop/terrace view → breakfast spread → pool/spa detail → reception smile.
- End line: **"Tu hotel merece llenarse así."** Music: dreamy, aspirational, slightly slower tempo.
- Shot list swaps: dish → habitación; comedor → terraza/rooftop; pour → desayuno/cóctel de bienvenida.

**Gym**
- Hook (0–1s): Higgsfield dynamic push-in on equipment / a dramatic dumbbell rack with hard side-light. Text: **"Esto es un gym de tu barrio."**
- Beats: weights close-up → someone training (energy) → group class → trainer high-five → clean facility wide.
- End line: **"Tu gym merece llenarse de socios."** Music: high-energy, driving beat, faster cuts.
- Shot list swaps: plato → equipamiento; comedor → sala de entrenamiento; chorro → gota de sudor / batido post-entreno; cliente disfrutando → socio motivado.

> **Reusable template:** Cinematic AI opener (animate their best still) → "Esto es un [negocio] de tu barrio" → 3–4 real clips cut to the beat → "¿Y si se viera así?" → end card "Tu [negocio] merece verse así." Swap the noun, swap the music energy, keep the structure.
