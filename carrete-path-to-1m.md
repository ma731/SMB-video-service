# Carrete: The Path to a €1M Business

Date: 2026-07-23 · Status: strategy draft, grounded in the launch-kit pricing

> **The uncomfortable truth first.** This repo has a complete MVP plan dated June 15 and, as of today, zero paying customers. A €1M plan written before venue #1 pays is fan fiction. So this doc does two things: (1) defines the €1M model so every early decision points at it, and (2) is brutally clear that steps 0-1 below are the only ones that matter this month.

---

## 1. What "€1M business" means (pick the target)

| Definition | What it takes | Realistic timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **€1M cumulative revenue** | ~€83k MRR-years total; a good 2-person agency gets here | 3-4 years grinding |
| **€1M ARR** (the real target) | **€83k MRR ≈ 175-210 active clients at €400-475 ARPA** | 24-36 months with the engine below |
| €1M valuation | ~€250-350k ARR of sticky recurring revenue (3-4x multiple on productized services) | 12-18 months |

Target: **€1M ARR**. At the launch-kit pricing (Growth anchor €499, blended ARPA ~€400 after Básico mix), that is roughly **200 venues**. Madrid alone has ~30,000 hospitality businesses, so the market is not the constraint. The constraints are, in order: proof, production capacity, sales capacity, churn.

---

## 2. Why the obvious paths fail (and what that teaches us)

**Path A: stay a 2-person agency.** Two people hand-producing videos cap out around 25-35 clients (~€12-15k MRR, €150-180k ARR). Nice income, not €1M, and it dies the month either founder stops.

**Path B: pivot to self-serve SaaS.** The market already answered this: PixelMotion is $29/mo, Seedance and a dozen others compete at $20-50/mo, and AI has cut video production costs 90%+. Two students in Madrid do not out-engineer that race to the bottom. The tooling is a commodity.

**The synthesis:** the commodity tooling is exactly why the *service* wins. Restaurant owners do not want a tool (they did not want Canva either; they wanted the menu done). Carrete's product is **"you never think about video again,"** and AI is the invisible cost engine that makes that promise deliverable at software-like margins. Sell the service, keep the SaaS margins.

## 3. The €1M architecture: three stacked layers

### Layer 1 (months 0-6): Prove and productize. Goal: 25 clients, €10k MRR
The current launch kit, executed, plus one change of mindset: **every video produced must also produce a reusable asset** (a prompt template, a hook formula, an editing preset, a niche-specific shot list). By client 25 the "factory" should turn 10 photos into a delivered reel in **under 45 minutes of human time**. Track that number weekly; it is the whole business.

- Unit economics to hit: €75-100 revenue per video, <€3 AI generation cost, <45 min labor. Gross margin >80%.
- Churn discipline from day 1: monthly report to each client ("views, saves, o cuántos vinieron por el vídeo"). Churn >7%/mo at this stage means the product is wrong; fix before scaling.

### Layer 2 (months 6-18): Scale production and sales separately. Goal: 80-100 clients, €35-45k MRR
- **Production:** hire 1-2 part-time editors (film students, €15-20/video piece rate) running the Carrete playbook. Founders stop editing; they own quality control and the template library. Capacity per editor: ~150 videos/mo.
- **Sales:** the sample-reel-plus-proposal wedge becomes a machine. One full-time-equivalent doing 10 outreach/day converts (at even 5% contact-to-paid) ~10-12 new clients/mo. Add the two channels that beat cold DMs:
  - **Partnerships:** TheFork/CoverManager account managers, POS vendors, hospitality associations, gestorías. They own trust with thousands of venues; give them 10-15% recurring referral.
  - **The flywheel:** every delivered reel ends with a subtle "vídeo: @carrete" credit. Client posts are the ad. Local business is a village; 30 venues in a barrio means everyone has seen your work.
- **Geographic clone:** the playbook (docs in this repo, literally) makes Barcelona/Valencia/Sevilla launchable by one salesperson + shared production pool. No office, no shoots (photos come by WhatsApp), so expansion is a hiring decision, not a capital one.

### Layer 3 (months 18-36): The leverage products. Goal: 200 clients equivalent, €83k+ MRR
By now Carrete owns two assets nobody selling $29 software has: **a library of hooks/templates proven to convert for Spanish hospitality**, and **distribution trust with hundreds of venue owners**. Monetize both:

1. **Carrete Autopilot (€99-149/mo tier):** mostly-automated plan where the pipeline (photo intake → template → AI generation → human 10-min QC) produces the reels with minimal touch. This is the SaaS margin layer, but sold as a service, through the existing brand. It opens the long tail of venues that will never pay €499.
2. **White-label for agencies (€ per video, volume):** hundreds of small Spanish marketing agencies have restaurant clients and no video capability. Carrete becomes their invisible video factory. One agency deal = 5-20 venues of volume with zero CAC.
3. **Chains and groups:** one hospitality group (10-40 locations) at a negotiated €250-350/location is €3-10k MRR from a single sales conversation. After ~50 independent-venue proofs, pitch groups directly.

**The €83k MRR composition that actually gets there:** ~90 direct service clients (€40k) + ~250 Autopilot subscribers (€28k) + 3-4 agency/chain deals (€15k). No single miracle required; three compounding engines.

## 4. Moat (why this survives better AI)

Better AI video models make Carrete *cheaper to run*, not easier to displace. The defensible assets, in order of value:
1. **Distribution and trust** with venue owners (relationships, referral network, the barrio flywheel).
2. **The conversion library:** which hooks fill tables in Spain. Every model has Veo; nobody has 5,000 delivered reels of local performance data.
3. **The brand promise:** "no pienses en vídeo nunca más." Tools cannot promise outcomes; services can.

---

## 5. What this changes about THIS WEEK (the only part that matters now)

Nothing in layers 2-3 gets built now. But the €1M frame changes three immediate behaviors:

1. **Run the validation sprint that has been pending since June 15.** Hero reel, 30-venue list, 10 DMs/day. The decision rule in the launch kit stands: 1 paying venue or a clear "no" within 2 weeks of starting.
2. **Time-track every video from day 1.** The path to €1M is the minutes-per-video curve going down while quality holds. If video #10 still takes 4 hours, the model is Path A (lifestyle agency), and that is worth knowing early.
3. **Write down every template as you go.** The template library is the future Autopilot product and the editor-hiring playbook. It costs nothing to capture now and everything to reconstruct later.

**Kill criteria stay honest:** 30+ venues contacted, samples and proposals sent, zero willingness to pay = the wedge is wrong. Change vertical (hotels? clinics with higher LTV?) or price before adding any layer-2 machinery.
