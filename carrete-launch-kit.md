# Carrete — Launch Kit

Done-for-you short-form video for local businesses in Madrid (restaurants, hotels, gyms, clinics). Two-person team: one sells, one produces. Goal of the next two weeks: **get one venue to pay.**

---

## 1. Brand

**Working name: Carrete** — Spanish for a film reel; short, memorable, on-theme. Alternatives if the domain/handle is taken: **Toma** (a film "take"), **Plano** (a camera shot), **Bobina**, **Reel Local**. Check `.studio` / `.es` / `.com` and the Instagram handle before committing.

- **One-liner (ES):** Vídeos que llenan tu negocio. Tú pones las fotos, nosotros el vídeo. En 48 horas.
- **One-liner (EN):** Short-form video for local businesses — you send photos, we make the reel, in 48 hours.
- **Positioning:** Not an "agency" (slow, expensive, scary) and not a freelancer (flaky). A simple, fixed-price monthly service that hands a busy owner ready-to-post Reels without cameras, shoots, or editing skills.
- **Who it's for (start narrow):** independent restaurants and cafés in 2–3 Madrid neighborhoods. Expand to hotels/gyms/clinics only after the restaurant motion works.

## 2. The offer & pricing

| Plan | Price/mo | Videos | For whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Básico** | **€149** | 2 | Price-sensitive / first-timers |
| Starter | €299 | 4 | Testing the waters |
| **Growth** (anchor) | **€499** | **8** | Most businesses — the one to push |
| Pro | €799 | 12 + monthly visit | Serious about social |

**Free first video.** Every first contact gets one free sample reel. This is the wedge that removes risk and gets you in the door — and it's your validation instrument.

**Why Básico is 2 videos (not "1 a week"):** at €149, four videos/month would mean ~€37/video and would undercut Starter — training customers to expect bargain pricing. Keeping Básico at 2 videos holds your per-video price at ~€75 across the bottom of the ladder (€75 → €75 → €62 at Growth as a volume discount). If you'd rather sell weekly cadence, price it at ~€199/mo for 4 videos instead. Prices are starting points — raise them once you have proof and testimonials. No lock-in; "cancela cuando quieras" lowers the barrier and is true.

## 3. How the service works (the production line)

1. **Intake (10 min):** owner sends 10–20 phone photos/clips of the space, dishes, or products via WhatsApp — or you do a quick 10-minute walkaround shoot on a phone gimbal.
2. **Produce (the AI-assisted part):**
   - Edit the real footage into a 15–25s vertical (9:16) reel in CapCut or Premiere.
   - Use **Kling 3.0** (~$0.10/sec, great value, strong motion) or **Veo 3.1** (best cinematic quality, native 4K + audio) to generate polished B-roll, smooth transitions, or an eye-catching opener from the owner's stills.
   - Add trending audio, captions, kinetic text, and a strong 1-second hook.
   - **Label AI-assisted content** per platform rules — and lean into "made with real footage of your place."
3. **Deliver (48 h):** send the MP4 ready to post, plus a suggested caption and best posting time.
4. **Tools:** Kling 3.0 / Veo 3.1 (generation) · CapCut or Premiere (edit) · an AI voiceover tool if needed · a shared Google Drive/Notion for assets. (Avoid building anything on Sora 2 — it's being deprecated in 2026.)

**Honest constraint:** you work *from the owner's real material*; you're enhancing and assembling, not inventing their venue. That's a feature — it stays authentic, which is exactly what audiences want in 2026.

## 4. Sample reel scripts (with AI prompts)

### A) Restaurant — "Dish hero" (15s)
- **Hook (0–1s):** Big text on a steaming/close-up dish: "El plato del que todo el barrio habla 👀"
- **Shots:** 0–3s extreme close-up of the dish (real photo, slow push-in) → 3–7s pan across 2–3 other dishes → 7–11s a happy table / interior ambience → 11–15s exterior + logo + "📍 [Barrio], Madrid · Reserva en bio".
- **Kling/Veo B-roll prompt:** *"Cinematic slow dolly push-in on a [dish] on a rustic wooden table, warm restaurant lighting, shallow depth of field, steam rising gently, photoreal, 9:16 vertical, 4 seconds."*
- **Caption (ES):** "Si todavía no has probado [plato], esto es una señal 😏 Te esperamos en [nombre]. 📍[zona]"

### B) Boutique hotel / Airbnb — "Wake up here" (20s)
- **Hook:** "Imagina despertarte aquí mañana ☀️"
- **Shots:** light coming through the window (real) → slow pan of the room → detail shots (bed, balcony, bathroom) → rooftop/views → "Reserva directa, sin comisiones".
- **Veo prompt:** *"Cinematic morning light sweeping across a cozy boutique hotel room, sheer curtains moving slightly, warm tones, slow camera pan, photoreal, 9:16, 5 seconds."*
- **Caption (ES):** "Tu próxima escapada en Madrid empieza aquí. Link para reservar en bio 🛎️"

### C) Gym / studio — "Energy" (15s)
- **Hook:** "Tu gimnasio no tiene por qué ser aburrido 🔥"
- **Shots:** fast cuts of equipment/classes (real) → one strong action moment → community/smiles → "1ª clase gratis esta semana".
- **Kling prompt:** *"Dynamic gym b-roll, dramatic side lighting, fast energetic camera moves across modern equipment, motivational mood, photoreal, 9:16, 4 seconds."*
- **Caption (ES):** "Primera clase gratis esta semana 💪 Ven a probar. 📍[zona]"

Build **one** of these as your hero sample before any outreach — ideally for the type of business you'll pitch first.

### Objection handling
- *"Ya tengo a alguien / lo hago yo."* → "Perfecto, por eso el primero es gratis: comparáis y decidís. Sin compromiso."
- *"No tengo tiempo."* → "De eso se trata: nos pasáis 10 fotos por WhatsApp y nosotros hacemos el resto."
- *"¿Cuánto cuesta?"* → "Desde 149 €/mes por 2 vídeos, sin permanencia. Pero el primero es gratis para que lo veáis."
- *"No sé si funciona."* → "Por eso medimos: subimos el vídeo, vemos si trae más consultas, y decidís con datos."

## 6. The 2-week validation plan (split between you two)

**Goal:** one paying venue, or clear proof of why not.

**Suggested split** (adjust to your co-founder's strengths — tell me what they're good at and I'll re-balance):
- **Founder A — Sales/relationships:** build the list, do all outreach, run the meetings, close.
- **Founder B — Production:** make the hero sample + every free trial video, own turnaround.

**Week 1 — Build + reach out**
- Day 1–2: pick the first vertical + 2–3 neighborhoods; finalize name + Instagram handle; publish the landing page.
- Day 2–3: produce 1 polished hero sample reel (your calling card).
- Day 3–7: list 30 target venues (Instagram + Google Maps). Send 10 outreach messages/day. Offer the free trial video.

**Week 2 — Deliver + close**
- Make free sample videos for everyone who says yes (aim for 5).
- 48 h after they post, follow up: "¿Qué tal funcionó? ¿Seguimos con un plan?"
- Ask for the sale. Track: # contacted → # replied → # free videos → # paid.

**Decision rule (end of week 2):**
- ≥1 paying venue, or strong "yes if you keep going" signals → continue and systematize.
- 30+ contacted, samples delivered, zero interest in paying → the offer or segment is wrong; change vertical or pricing before quitting.

**First action today:** finalize the name + handle, and Founder B starts the hero sample. Founder A builds the 30-venue list.
